5. Statistics and interpretation
For each seed compute100*(context_correct-direct_correct)/n on identical items.
Primary t95 = mean +/-2.570581835636314*sampleSD/sqrt(6), df5; report six values.
Intervals describe training-seed variation conditional on target cache/test items,
not independent-question uncertainty. Do not pool six seeds as six times n items.
Also context-base/direct-base, first/second-hop retention, and person/country
cluster bootstrap (2000 replicates, seed12014) as secondary sensitivity conditional
on fitted seeds. Exact paired McNemar tests: clean composed18 tests (3x6) Holm;
clean retention36 tests (3x6x2) Holm. These do not replace primary paired t95.
Teacher same-question advantage and allfour prompt/target NLL combinations retain
exp12 diagnostics. NLL excludes prompt and EOS; raw PPL across different answers
is not factuality. Hold target string fixed for prompt-effect comparisons.
Export v3 repairs AND damages against own baseline with blind answer-pair audit;
manual semantics fields stay empty. A successful computation is not a completed
manual audit. Do not automatically classify flips as content/format changes.

Positive lower CI: cross-family directional efficacy replication under this task,
training and scorer; teacher-quality explanation and mechanism not thereby ruled
out. Negative upper CI: opposite effect. CI including0: inconclusive, not proven
absence/equivalence. Always discuss size, review sensitivity, baseline gains,
retention, sample coverage and token/training costs with the primary decision.
No extra seeds, rounds, controls, model tuning or follow-up experiments here.
