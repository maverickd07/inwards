Scoring and endpoints
Vendor unchanged official metrics/answer.py and metrics/metric.py from frozen commit. Use official normalize_answer, maximum-over-official-aliases EM/F1 on the full decoded answer. Keep full numerical precision; upstream display rounding is not a scoring rule. Empty outputs and truncation are scored by the same fixed rule; report counts separately, no selective exclusion.
PRIMARY: context-minus-direct composed EM on ALL fixed1229 two-hop questions. Six paired training-seed effects, mean and Student t95 df5 (2.570581835636314). Also report context-minus-base and direct-minus-base; base evaluated once and reused for all pairs. Positive-transfer claim requires context-direct AND context-base t95 lower bounds >0. Context beating direct while both degrade does not establish successful transfer.
SECONDARY/DESCRIPTIVE: two-hop F1; three-hop, four-hop and pooled EM/F1; all oracle hops correct; per-hop canonical/alias EM and F1; truncation/think flags. Do not substitute a favorable secondary for a null primary. Intervals reflect training-seed variability on this fixed pool, not question sampling, gold uncertainty or pretraining overlap.
Before any adapter inference, derive and save baseline-only strata: (a) all oracle single-hop answers EM-correct, (b) all correct but composed EM-wrong. Score adapter composed EM on those SAME baseline-fixed identities. Main pool remains unchanged. Mark known strata <100 items as small/descriptive; never stop or switch datasets based on accuracy, claim a floor-adjusted primary, or define a different known subset for each model.

Interpretation fixed before results
1. Primary context-direct and context-base lower bounds >0: evidence for positive transfer in this predefined closed-book setting; not mechanism proof or general multi-hop capability claim.
2. Context-direct positive but context-base not established: relative advantage only; do not call it successful transfer over base.
3. Primary interval crossing zero: inconclusive/no clear advantage, not equivalence and not rejection of all inward-learning methods.
4. Primary context-direct upper bound <0: report negative relative transfer. Check context-base separately to distinguish damage from relative inferiority.
5. Few baseline-known items or weak single-hop performance: describe knowledge/diagnostic limits; retain primary result and do not erase unfavorable outcomes by filtering.
6. Existing45 training-label ambiguities and differing target token budgets remain unresolved. Cross-domain transfer does not settle those causal confounds.
End after13 model evaluations; no automatic extra seeds, datasets, prompt variants, or training. Preserve every prior result.
